About Advantage Financial

Our Mission

Advantage Financial Services provides accessible financing and leasing solutions to fuel growth, stability, and long-term success for our vendors, customers, commercial entities and municipalities.

Our Mission

We believe financing should be straightforward, accessible and built around relationships. Our goal is to provide financing and leasing solutions that help businesses move forward with confidence.

Our Founding

Experience Built Around Relationships

Established in January of 2001, Advantage Financial Services, LLC provides customized vendor-focused lease and financing solutions with a focus on information technology and related products and solutions.

Industries we focus on include Office Equipment, Printing, Medical, Pharmacy, Recreational, Manufacturing and Material Handling.
